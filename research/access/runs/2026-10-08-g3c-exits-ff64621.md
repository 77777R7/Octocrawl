- Switching is bounded: shown. After the third failure the task did not switch (two switches is the cap); `a=9` and `a=10` failed with `connection_error` on the egress it was on.
- A 429 is never answered by switching egress: shown again (`a=3`, `blocked`, no switch; the next page stayed on the same egress until that egress itself was closed).
