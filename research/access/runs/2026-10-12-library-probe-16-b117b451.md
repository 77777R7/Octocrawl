## What the arms show

- **Every arm refused, 13 of 16.** The seven Cloudflare tasks (T001, T047, T048, T068, T071, T076, B001) and the six DataDome tasks (T005, T034, T038, T045, T046, T049) answer 403 with challenge wording to every client from this exit, the two browsers included; Patchright differs from stock Playwright on none of them. B008 (imdb) answers a 202 shell under 2 kB to the HTTP clients and 403 to both browsers. From a residential exit, a browser-compatible TLS stack, a real Chromium and Patchright's patches change none of these answers; what these vendors hold on is not the exit, the transport fingerprint or the marks Patchright removes.
- **T002 (ember-energy.org): every arm passes.** The product's standard route was refused through the forward proxy's data-centre exit and passed through this gateway in arm C: the block is the exit's reputation, nothing else.
- **T053 (imf.org): both browsers pass, every HTTP client is refused.** The product's browser rung was refused through the same gateway in arm C (`bot_detected_generic`, 403), where a stock Playwright context here got the page. What differs between them is the product's own browser rung (its identity overrides, headers and settle), not the exit or the engine: a hypothesis for G2, cheap to test, and the one task of the 16 where a change on Octocrawl's side could count.
- Two arm errors are failures, not evidence: impit + Octocrawl UA on T071 did not connect; Playwright on T076 could not read the page after its navigation was interrupted by the challenge.
