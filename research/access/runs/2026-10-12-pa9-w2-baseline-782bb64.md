## Reading, not isolated

As in window 1, the difference sits on sites with bot defences: on the healthy controls Octocrawl verified every task, more than either competitor. The same day's experiment on the 23 tasks a competitor verified in window 1 and Octocrawl did not ([D](2026-10-11-pa4-3arm-d-control-e3b9d37.md), [A](2026-10-11-pa4-3arm-a-residential-e3b9d37.md), [B](2026-10-11-pa4-3arm-b-residential-solve-6021174.md), [C](2026-10-11-pa4-3arm-c-webshare-d65614d.md)) found that neither a residential exit nor a provider's CAPTCHA solving moves most of them: the pages are Cloudflare challenge pages and bot checks, which the competitors pass and Octocrawl's routes do not. That, not the exit, is the gap the bar names.
