# T031 (Temu's home) read five times, 2026-10-13

Why: on 2026-10-12 T031 was a false success in the 92-task run at `48fd6e17` ([record](2026-10-12-pa4-92-main-48fd6e17.md)). The local browser's answer was Temu's "Sign in / Register" overlay, 1,299 characters with no product, taken as content. These reads look at what Temu serves this exit, and whether a login wall can be told from the page.

- Network: proxied (`HTTPS_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:7890`). Exit address not recorded.
- Run: 2026-10-12, 16:57–16:59Z (00:57–00:59 on 2026-10-13 in the operator's time zone, which the file is named after).
- Two reads through the task runner, the task's own request and contract:
  - `node research/access/run-set.mjs --set all --only T031 --access "standard" --raw-html --save-responses`, twice. With `--record` left out, the runs are under `.w2l/access/runs/2026-10-12T16-57-46-206Z` and `…T16-58-01-057Z`.
- Three direct scrapes of the whole page:
  - `POST /v1/scrape` with `{"url":"https://www.temu.com/","formats":["rawHtml","markdown"],"onlyMainContent":false,"maxAge":0}`, three times.

- None of the five was the sign-in overlay of 2026-10-12. Temu answered this exit four different ways over two days: a page with its products (T031 was verified on 2026-10-11), a home without products, a sign-in overlay, and its verification page. The task stays unstable, as the set marks it.
- The login wall cannot be told by its words. A full home page carries "sign in" 73 times and "register" 75 times, its header's "Sign in / Register" among them, and no password field. A rule on the words would refuse Temu's ordinary home. Not changed.
- The verification page is told by its address: `bgn_verification.html` with a `verifyCode`, reached by a redirect from the home. The gate does not see the final URL (`GateResponse` carries the status, headers and body), so it is not classified. Not changed here.
- The verification page was `success` because a page of zero-width characters counted as text. The extractor's visible-text count and the whole page's emptiness check (`markdown.trim()`) both keep U+200B, which JavaScript's `\s` and `trim()` do not remove. Fixed in the PR that adds this record: such a page is `failed`/`empty_unverified` with `textChars` 0.

Suspected cause of the varying answers: not isolated (the exit's reputation and Temu's own checks are both candidates).
