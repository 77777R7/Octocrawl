# T048: raw CDP against Playwright over CDP, and the product, 2026-10-15 (a negative result)

Label: `library_probe` for the two probes, `product` for the product's runs. On 2026-10-13, with the same narrowed Chrome launch on the Clash exit, raw CDP passed T048 (crunchbase.com, Cloudflare) twice and Playwright over CDP failed it twice ([record](2026-10-14-library-probe-pwcdp.md)). That record could not tell a driver effect from T048's own variation.

The product drives installed_chrome with Playwright over CDP, so TASKS item 7 asked whether a minimal raw-CDP reader is worth adding to it. On this evidence it is not.

## How it was run

- **Machine and Chrome.** Howard's Mac (arm64), installed Google Chrome 155.0.8059.40 headed, the Clash exit `127.0.0.1:7890`. Main at `c9679e8f`. T048 alone, from `research/access/tasks.v1.json`: "OpenAI" and "Founded" on its page.
- **Raw CDP:** `ALLOW_ORIGINS=none RANDOM_PORT=1 PROBE_PROXY=… node research/access/probe-cdp.mjs`, the narrowed launch.
- **Playwright over CDP:** `PROBE_PROXY=… node research/access/probe-pwcdp.mjs`, the same launch driven by `connectOverCDP`. It now runs on the repository's own Playwright, 1.62.1, as the product does; on 2026-10-13 it ran on a scratch install of 1.64.
- **Both probes judge the page's visible text by the task's predicates,** the same way.
- **The product:** the local API with a grant naming `installed_chrome` alone. The command was `run-set.mjs --set all --only T048 --access '"enhanced"' --save-responses`, judged by the product's own verification.
- **Two rounds, each alternating its arms one attempt at a time:**
  - raw CDP and Playwright over CDP, 6 each, 00:06–00:10;
  - the product and raw CDP, 5 each, 00:11–00:13.
- **Raw outputs, outside the repository:** `.w2l/access/archive/2026-10-15/t048-driver`. The probes' JSON and logs, plus the product's run directories `2026-10-14T16-11-05-561Z` to `16-13-16-273Z` (UTC).

- **The Playwright probe failed the same way all six times:** a 403, "Attention Required! | Cloudflare".
- **Raw CDP's failures** were that page twice and "Just a moment..." once in round 1, and twice in round 2.
- **The product's three failures** were the same 403 after its 19 s challenge wait. Its two passes cleared the challenge in about 3 s.

## What this shows

- **The product does not do worse than raw CDP in the same minutes.** The product passed 2 of 5 against raw CDP's 3 of 5. Both arms' passes fell in the round's last minutes (from 00:13): the time of the attempt moved T048 more than the driver did.
- **The Playwright probe's 0 of 6 is that probe's, not the product's.** In the same minutes raw CDP passed half the time, and the product, which also drives Chrome through Playwright over CDP, passed too. What the probe does differently was not found.
  - The 2026-10-13 lead compared the same probe, so it does not speak for the product either.
- **No raw-CDP reader is added.** Nothing here shows a gain the product would get from one. T048 stays one of the tasks whose outcome depends on Cloudflare's call at the time.
- **For later A/Bs,** the passes clustering in time is a reason to alternate arms attempt by attempt, as both rounds here did.
