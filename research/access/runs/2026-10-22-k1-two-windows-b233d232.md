## What held in both windows

- **installed_chrome is the strongest Octocrawl arm in both windows**, and its gain over standard is the only large one that held:
  - 60 and 68 first-call verified;
  - 15 tasks gained in both windows over standard, 0 regressions, $0 by the ledger (no paid route; the Clash exit's bandwidth is not measured: unknown exposure);
  - gap to the best competitor (Firecrawl): 9, then 2;
  - false successes 2 and 1 of 92.
- **enhanced (Steel datacenter) did not hold.**
  - 56 in window 1, 49 in window 2: in window 2, 12 first calls timed out at the Steel rung.
  - Only 3 gains held in both windows (T003, T007, T018), against 1 regression (T008), with 14 tasks unstable against standard.
  - First-call cost $0.0673 and $0.0731 (ledger).
  - Its p95 rose from 36 s to 166 s.
- **Firecrawl leads both windows, 69 and 70 verified, and keeps 12 false successes in each** (24/184, upper bound 17.9%).
  - Its gain over standard (28 held) also carries 7 regressions held in both windows, where Octocrawl standard verifies and Firecrawl does not.
  - ZenRows: 55 and 59, with 20 and 18 false successes.
- **False successes across both windows (of 184):** Octocrawl arms 3–6, Firecrawl 24, ZenRows 38.
- **The healthy-25 done criterion is not met in either window.** The candidate is one below the pinned control both times, on the same task:
  - T016: a `TypeError` in the `browser_local` rung, reported as `connection_error`, on all three current-commit arms, six of six;
  - verified on `2ffd7f25` both times;
  - details in window 2's notes.
  - **T016 is a known regression, already fixed on `main`.** Both K1 windows deliberately pinned runner packages equal to `322de68e` (the engine of `main` `7ba113c9`), so both predate the fix. The cause is in #671 (merged 2026-10-22 as `33f2c174`, `extract-tf/35`): Zara's JSON-LD ItemList declares Products with `"name": ""`. After #628, `declare()` stored a null fact, and `keysOf()` read `.value` of it. The extraction throw was reported as `connection_error`; #679 (`dbb1fbab`) reports such a crash as `internal_error` / `extraction_failed`. #671 reports T016 verified 4/4 on the live site on its branch, proxied, access `standard`; this session did not rerun it. The healthy-25 shortfall in both windows is that regression. The measured numbers above stay as measured.
- **No default route is changed by this record.** K4 decides, citing the table below.
