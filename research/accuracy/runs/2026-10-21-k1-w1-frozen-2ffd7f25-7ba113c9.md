**What this measures and what it does not:**
- These are saved bytes. They say nothing about live access, routing or freshness; those are the live records of this window.
- v2 has been regression data since #639. The selectors were written for this run with the page in view, which is how a user would write them. So the list numbers measure the selected-list path, not unaided extraction.
- The engine's selector subset has no `:nth-child` and no sibling combinators. That is why several fields were left out (see the file), and it caps what this path can deliver on tables with identical cells.
