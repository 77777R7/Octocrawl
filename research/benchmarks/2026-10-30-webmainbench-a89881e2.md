# WebMainBench: Octocrawl's extractor after PR #778, 2026-10-30

A record of one run: the Octocrawl conversions of the morning's WebMainBench record
(`research/benchmarks/2026-10-30-webmainbench-2aaad1f4.md`) made again with the engine of PR #778, on the same stored
HTML, and scored the same way. The comparators were not run again: none of their code changed. No network request, no
paid service and no LLM.

## What ran

- Octocrawl source commit: `a89881e2` (branch `claude/extractor-benchmark-fixes`, PR
  https://github.com/77777R7/Octocrawl/pull/778, not merged), `origin/main` at `f6a75df5` plus that PR; no engine
  package differs between `f6a75df5` and the baseline's `2aaad1f4`. Of the PR's three changes only one reaches this
  benchmark: the product region's walk up from the heading (`extract-tf/47`). Engine build: up to date
  (`tsc --build --dry`, 2026-10-29 20:49:44Z), the tree clean.
- Dataset, scorers, Python environment: as in the baseline record (HF revision `5da0972e…`; MinerU-HTML `73cf2666`;
  WebMainBench toolkit `9d991bdc`). The glue ran from a copy of `research/benchmarks/webmainbench/` as of `e3fd73c7`,
  outside the checkout, which was on the engine branch.
- Commands: those of the baseline record with `W=.w2l/benchmarks/runs/wmb-2026-10-30-post`, after
  `git checkout a89881e2 && npx tsc --build`, without `--trafilatura` and with `--with ""` (Octocrawl's views only).
  Conversion of the 7,809 pages took 2 min 27 s.
