## Outline

Title: **How to Find All Pages on a Website: 6 Ways (Free)**

1. **Answer first** (40–60 words), listing the six ways and which is fastest. Entry card to the tool.
2. **Six ways**, each with when it works and its limit:
   1. **The sitemap, found from robots.txt.** modelcontextprotocol.io's robots.txt names `/sitemap.xml`, which lists 357 URLs.
   2. **Google `site:`.** `site:modelcontextprotocol.io` says "About 4,310 results", and its first five results are all on subdomains (blog., ts.sdk., php.sdk., ruby.sdk.); `site:modelcontextprotocol.io/specification` says about 192 where the sitemap lists 142. `site:octocrawl.dev` says about 26. The count is Google's estimate and `site:` includes subdomains.
   3. **Search Console (your own site only).** For octocrawl.dev, URL Inspection on the 24 URLs the map found: 21 "Submitted and indexed", 3 "URL is unknown to Google" (`/scrape/`, `/llms.txt`, `/signin/`).
   4. **A crawler** (Screaming Frog and similar). Not run for this brief; limits from the vendor's own page.
   5. **Map it in one call** (Octocrawl). modelcontextprotocol.io: 359 URLs in 3.3 s (357 from the sitemap, 18 from start-page links, 2 found only by links: `/` and `/llms.txt`), 18 duplicates and 8 other-host links counted. octocrawl.dev: 24 URLs, 22 from the sitemap. Keep the first version's `via`, `sitemapFile`, `lastmod`, `refused` and option table here. Second entry point.
   6. **Archives** (Wayback Machine CDX, Common Crawl index) for pages that used to exist. Wayback: 582 HTML URLs with a 200 capture; Common Crawl CC-MAIN-2026-39: 411.
3. **Which way to use**: table by own site or not, sitemap or not, JavaScript-built links, need status codes.
4. **How to check the list is complete** (the gap no ranking page fills). Compare sources, then request what only one source has. On modelcontextprotocol.io the archives held 458 URLs the map did not; 447 were requested (11 under `/_next/`, which robots.txt disallows, were not): 94 returned 404, 96 redirected (81 of them to a page the map listed), 1 failed, and 256 returned 200. Of the 256, 203 were `.md` copies of listed pages, the rest `/index` aliases and `.md` copies of those, and one `robots.txt`. **No archive-only URL was a live page the map missed.** The point: a longer list is not a more complete one until each extra URL is checked.
5. **Pages no method finds**: orphan pages, pages behind a login, links built by JavaScript (the map flags `start_page_client_rendered`; a browser scrape reads them).
6. **FAQ**: the four existing questions plus "How many pages does a website have?", "How do I find all subpages of a website?", "Why isn't every page in the sitemap?", answering the People also ask questions above.
