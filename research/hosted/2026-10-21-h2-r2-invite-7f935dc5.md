## State left

- Hosted jobs are on, for Howard's account only.
- Any other account gets 403 `jobs_invite_only` and is not shown the job tools.
- **To invite someone:** add their uid to `allowedUids`. Each needs `users/{uid}`, so they must have signed in once. The services read the list at most every 30 s, and no deploy is needed.
- **To open jobs to every account:** remove the field.
- **To pause jobs:** set `enabled` to false.
