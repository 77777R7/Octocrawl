## The four that were weak

### scrape-formats.markdown: solid

- **Table pages keep every data table.** On the previous record's fixture (a nav header, the `<h1>` in a banner `<div>`, a content `<div>` with a one-line intro and three captioned data tables of 10, 8 and 6 rows under `<h2>` headings), `POST /v1/scrape` (in-process API, HTTP lane) on `6024703` gives `success`, strategy `table`, 998 characters: all three tables, the three headings and the intro, no navigation. On `5bfffec` it was the first table alone (392 characters). `extractTf.extract` gives the same three tables with the `<h1>` in a banner, directly in `<body>`, or absent. Real site: M12 (EIA Electric Power Annual table 1.1: the title, two data tables, notes and sources) 10/10.
- **Table cells keep link targets.** `htmlToMarkdown` turns `<td><a href="https://example.org/story">A story</a></td>` into `| [A story](https://example.org/story) |`, and a `|` in a cell's text or target into `\|`. Real site: M13, 210 of 210 targets in Hacker News' story table absolute.
- **The docs.** README.md:152 states the nested-table rule the code has and what a table page keeps. The station-table fixture (12 rows and a summary row whose last cell holds a 2×2 table) is one GFM grid with `a 1 b 2` as that cell's text.
- The uncounted `data:` link target of the previous record is closed too: the link keeps its text and loses its target.
- Remaining, not counted: the `waitFor`-deadline Markdown drop, the CLI excerpt, block-laid-out spans on the HTTP lane, emphasis in cells written as plain text (documented). A page that is nothing but a table of links is `failed`/`empty_unverified` (a local 12-row link table), which is not the table fix's scope; M13 covers the real page.
