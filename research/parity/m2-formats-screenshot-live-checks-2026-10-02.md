## Deviations from the plan, stated

- The plan foresaw that Chromium might refuse or clip a full-page capture past 16,384 px and asked for the limit to be measured. It was not found: 16,400 (scratch), 20,150 (C1), 100,150 (C2) and 400,150 px (C8) captured whole, with `captureMs` growing roughly with the height (316 ms, 1,047 ms, 4,117 ms) and the PNG to 4.2 MB. So no live check reached the `screenshot_failed` / `screenshot_unavailable` path; it is covered by a unit test with a stubbed page whose `screenshot` rejects (`packages/bench/test/screenshot.test.ts`), and the hint by `packages/api/test/hints.test.ts`. A response with a 400,000 px capture is 5.8 MB of JSON; the README and the batch doc say viewport captures are the lighter choice for crawls and batches.
- S3 was run as the plan's second sample for `scrape-formats.screenshot` because S1 passed; the Apple fallback for the full-page row was not run because S2 passed. No Apple fetch, no fourth real site.
- The Wikipedia row's planner note expected "243 table rows still in the markdown" from the earlier `excludeTags` check; this check sent no `excludeTags`, and a plain count of GFM table lines (every `|`-leading line that is not a separator) gives 271, headers included. The table content is there; the two counts measure different things.
- C8 was not in the plan; it was added after C2 captured 100,000 px whole, to see whether a ceiling exists at all. One loopback fetch.
- The viewport check of the identity (`identityBundleIssues` on the effective bundle) is recorded by the `screenshot_viewport` trace event (S1, S3, C4), as the plan asked; a viewport the identity cannot take (larger than the mobile identity's 412x915 screen) is refused by the API before any fetch (`packages/contracts/test/api.test.ts`) and was not sent live.

## What the run also showed

1. Every capture is CSS-pixel sized at `deviceScaleFactor` 2: `scale: "css"` does what the plan intended (the scratch measurement shows 2560x1600 with `scale: "device"` for the same page).
2. Wikipedia renders on the browser lane through the proxy (S2: `navigate` once, 2.3 s round trip), which earlier records had left unverified since StatCan's navigation had timed out that way.
3. Energy-Charts' markdown is its carousel captions, as the frozen row predicted; the chart itself is in the capture only.
4. The `/fc` response of C7 carries `links: []` beside `screenshot`, as every `/fc` scrape response carries links (a known diff of the shim, not of this batch).
