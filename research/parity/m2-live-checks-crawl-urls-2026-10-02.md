## Deviations from the plan, stated

- B1 reports `collapsed 6`, not 24: the `?per_page=100` page carries six `page_num` links (the plan's 24 is the 25-row page's pagination). Every one of the six merges is named in the samples.
- B2's two followed pages were recorded page 2 before page 1: they are the first two links in the page's order, fetched by two workers at once and written as each finished.
- D2 reached `/3/whatsnew/changelog.html`, a 3.6 MB page that offered 12,643 links (12,318 of them to other hosts); the crawl stayed within its budget of 10 pages.
- E1's two asset pages were a PDF and an XLSX, the first two asset links in document order; the `.ods` the plan mentions was enqueued and not fetched within `maxPages 3`.
- The whole-crawl `discovery` totals add the depth-1 pages' own links (`depthDenied`, and on docs.python.org `duplicate` and `hostDenied`) to the seed's counts; the plan's per-crawl numbers are the seed page's `links_offered` event, which matched.
