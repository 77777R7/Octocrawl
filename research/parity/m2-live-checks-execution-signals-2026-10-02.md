## Deviations from the plan, stated

- A: the canonical URL is `metadata.canonicalUrl` on main, where the plan wrote `metadata.canonical`; the value is the one expected.
- B: the waits are about 5.8 s and 9.0 s, above the plan's floors of 2 s and 4 s, because each fetch held the slot about 3 s through the proxy rather than 2 s.
- D: `markdown` is the 663-character denial page, where the plan wrote null. The test set's row 18 asks that the denial page not come back as content; it comes back on a `blocked` result as evidence, which is how main keeps every non-2xx page (CHANGELOG: "A non-2xx response keeps its page as evidence ... while its status stays failed or blocked"), and the hints do not quote it. Recorded as observed; whether a block page should be kept belongs to the error model.
- E: `retryAfterSeconds` was 56, the time left in the sliding minute at the third call; the plan's range of 1 to 60 000 ms for `retryAfterMs` held (56 000).
