  - **Unconfirmed:** why the refusal came later. It may be a delay on Airbnb's side, or the sign-out may have completed only after step 3. The time between the steps was not recorded.
- **Steps 1 and 2 also show #197:** the login's SHA-256 is the same in the import's answer and in the read's record. In the group B run they differed.
