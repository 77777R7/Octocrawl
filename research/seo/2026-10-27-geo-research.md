# GEO research for Octocrawl (2026-10-27)

A dated record of one research run on generative engine optimisation (GEO): how AI assistants find, cite and recommend products, and what that means for Octocrawl. It is not edited afterwards; a later run gets its own dated file. The decisions that use it are in [docs/seo/core-seo-framework.md](../../docs/seo/core-seo-framework.md) and the living files in [docs/seo/library/](../../docs/seo/library/).

- Run on 2026-10-27 (the live-site checks in section 8 and the browser access check in section 11 on 2026-10-28), one Mac, source commit `30521c5b` plus the working tree. `curl`, `gh` calls went through the shell proxy (`HTTPS_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:7890`); `WebSearch` and `WebFetch` did not. Pages that blocked a tool are listed as blocked, not bypassed.
- 25 agents in one workflow: 8 GEO research agents in 4 groups and 10 prompt-drafting agents, each group followed by a verifier that tried to refute a sample (4 research verifiers, 3 prompt verifiers).
- Evidence grades: A = peer reviewed or a reproducible method with data and a stated sample; B = a named organisation with method and sample stated; C = anecdote, secondary report or single example. Vendors who sell AI-visibility tracking, PR or SEO software are marked: they have an interest in the result.
- Nothing here measures Octocrawl's own visibility in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Gemini. No such measurement was run (sections 10 and 11).

Guidance statements:

- **What to allow in robots.txt to appear in ChatGPT search**: Allow OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt and allow requests from OpenAI's published IP ranges (so a CDN/WAF does not block it). Sites that opt out of OAI-SearchBot are not shown in ChatGPT search answers but may still appear as navigational links. Changes can take about 24 hours. (https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)

### 3.4 Interval width for a mention rate (recomputed here)

Wilson 95% interval width in percentage points, plug-in p-hat = true rate, runs of one prompt treated as independent. This is a floor: runs of the same prompt are not independent samples of prompts. Computed with `python3 -I` from the standard Wilson formula; it equals the agent's table cell for cell.

| True mention rate | 3 runs | 10 runs | 30 runs | 100 runs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10% | 63.6 | 38.6 | 22.2 | 11.9 |
| 20% | 68.8 | 45.3 | 27.8 | 15.5 |
| 30% | 72.3 | 49.5 | 31.2 | 17.7 |
| 50% | 74.9 | 52.7 | 33.7 | 19.2 |

Applied to the 2026-10-16 Google AI Mode sample (one run per question): 6 of 12 named is 25.4% to 74.6%; 0 of 12 official citations is 0.0% to 24.2%. Zero successes in 60 runs bounds the rate below 6.0%; in 100 runs, below 3.7%.

## 7. Coverage of an external prompt library against Octocrawl

Howard supplied an observation prompt library and method pages written by another team. It is confidential to its author. Only the structure and the method were used. No prompt, wording, topic name or count from it appears in this record or in the Octocrawl library, and its topic breakdown is deliberately not summarised, because the breakdown would identify the other product.

- Most of that library covers topics outside what Octocrawl does (anything that needs a browser to be controlled), so those topics were left out of scope. Only a minority overlaps with Octocrawl's scope (page reading, source tracing, change monitoring).

## 9. Octocrawl prompt library: how it was drafted and checked

Ten agents each drafted prompts for one topic (12 to 16 each; 3 for page-change monitoring, where evidence was thin) from three allowed sources only: the real Search Console queries, the Hacker News and GitHub items from the [2026-10-27 research record](2026-10-27-core-seo-framework-research.md), and the Google and Bing autocomplete strings and SERP clusters in that record. Reddit supplied nothing (blocked). 144 rows were drafted; three verifiers re-checked every row against the evidence files and the capability boundary and flagged 43 as mismatches. 20 were dropped (near-duplicates, one reference that does not exist, one row with no demand evidence) and the rest were corrected field by field (modes, should-mention values, references, facts to check). The author then re-checked all 322 provenance references against the evidence files: all exist.

Results (124 prompts): 27 "should be mentioned", 70 "conditional on mode or limits", 27 "should not be mentioned" (calibration controls). After the second review round, 3 rows were moved from "should be mentioned" to "conditional" and several fact checks were corrected against the repository documents. Provenance kinds: Hacker News 85, autocomplete 82, GitHub 59, SERP cluster 34, Search Console 29, framework 18, ai-visibility 15. Search Console backs few rows because nearly all its queries are one family ("find all pages of a website"); Reddit backs none.

Verifier counts: batch 1: 44 confirmed, 17 mismatch; batch 2: 24 confirmed, 10 mismatch; batch 3: 32 confirmed, 16 mismatch.

Not tested: Claude in Chrome (the person's own Chrome, whose sign-ins may differ); whether a signed-out ChatGPT or Perplexity answers a question (on 2026-10-16 a signed-out Perplexity asked to sign up and a signed-in ChatGPT returned "Model not found", one question, one attempt each); the network exit of the built-in browser (the 2026-10-16 record also did not establish a US exit); repeatability (one check at one time).

Consequence: no measurement of Octocrawl in these assistants has been run. Collecting needs a signed-in web session that the person provides or authorises. A personal account carries memory and personalisation, so the protocol's temporary-chat settings apply and the result describes a user without history.
